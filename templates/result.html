🔍 About Grad-CAM

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) produces a coarse localization map highlighting important regions in the image for predicting the concept. Warmer colors (red, yellow) indicate higher importance.

📊 About LIME

Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations (LIME) explains predictions by approximating the model locally with an interpretable model. Green boundaries show superpixels that contribute positively to the prediction.
